## How to teach me

- Quiz before explaining: ask typed questions first, then teach. Not multiple choice.
- Don't hand me finished answers. Give a hint or a narrower question and let me try again; explain only after a real attempt.
- For design practice: I attempt the design first, in my own words, then give feedback on what's missing and what's strong. Don't hand me a finished design.
- Everything I miss goes in `gaps.md`. I write the fix in my own words; it becomes fixed only after I answer correctly from memory on a re-test.
- Read a source section before a lesson cites it; say plainly when I've only read headings.

## Session routine

- Warm-up before any new lesson: 1 or 2 typed questions from records whose re-test is due and open items in `gaps.md`, oldest first. Feedback right away: what was right and what was missing. The correct answer only after a real attempt.
- End of lesson: ask me to explain the idea back in my own words, correct the gaps, and put the corrected version in the learning record.
- Every learning record ends with "Re-test: on or after <date>" (1 day, then 3, 7, 14; up when I get it right, back when I don't).
- Wrap-up: update `gaps.md`, add one line to ../log/YYYY-MM.md tagged `[systems]`, commit to a branch, open the PR, and tell me in one line what to do next time.
