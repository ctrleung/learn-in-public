# Mission: Survival Japanese for a short trip to Japan (April 2027)

## Why
I'm going to Japan for a short trip next year and I don't know any Japanese yet. By April 2027 I want enough to read signs, get around, talk to locals and ask questions, so the trip is easier and more fun.

## Success looks like
- Read hiragana and katakana without a chart, so I can sound out signs, menus and station names
- Recognize about a dozen kanji I'll actually meet on signs and in stations, as shapes to match and read, not to write (candidate list in `RESOURCES.md`, to confirm)
- Get around: ask where something is, understand short directions, buy tickets and food, and handle numbers, prices and times
- Talk to locals: greet, introduce myself, make polite requests, order, ask "how much?", "do you have...?", and say I don't understand or ask someone to speak slowly
- Ask a question and understand a short answer, both heard (Genki app audio) and typed

## Constraints
- Short sessions, about 10 minutes or less; uneven energy, and the web plan runs at the same time until 14 Feb
- Trip is in April 2027; the exact date isn't fixed yet
- Materials: Genki textbook, workbook and app (audio), Anki for vocabulary, Renshuu for hiragana
- Typed chat can't check pronunciation, so audio practice happens in the Genki app

## Out of scope
- Learning kanji broadly, or writing kanji; only a small set to recognize on signs
- Business or formal Japanese, and test prep (such as the JLPT)
- Writing by hand, unless I decide I want it
