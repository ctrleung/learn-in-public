★ marks the lessons that look most useful on a trip. That's my reading of the titles, so adjust it. The order follows Genki.

## Why these lessons ★ for a trip

From the titles only: greetings, numbers, time and prices (Lessons 1 and 2), pointing and asking where (Lessons 2 and 4), "is there…?" (Lesson 4), suggesting (Lesson 3), requests and asking permission (Lesson 6), counting people for a restaurant (Lesson 7), and saying what you want (Lesson 11). The rest follow in Genki's order if time allows.
