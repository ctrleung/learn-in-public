### Not yet read (found by search, travel blogs and guides)

Candidates for the sign and station kanji list. These are travel blogs, not authoritative sources; no source found gives an official number of kanji a tourist needs, and the guides disagree on which to prioritize. Read them before a lesson cites them.

- [A few kanji to know for traveling in Japan (Japan Experience)](https://www.japan-experience.com/plan-your-trip/to-know/japanese-language/a-few-kanji-to-know-for-traveling-in-japan)
  Suggests memorizing 駅 (station) to match against maps. Use for: a short list of station and travel kanji.
- [Kanji characters that will help you survive Japan (Culture Trip)](https://theculturetrip.com/asia/japan/articles/kanji-characters-that-will-help-you-survive-japan)
  A list of survival kanji. Use for: cross-checking the candidate list.
- [Live Japan](https://livejapan.com/en/article-a0001251) and [All About Japan](https://allabout-japan.com/en/article/4836)
  Further sign and station kanji guides. Use for: cross-checking.
- [WaniKani community: a trip to Japan and language](https://community.wanikani.com/t/question-about-a-trip-to-japan-regarding-language/33235)
  Forum discussion of how much language a trip needs. Use for: perspective only.

**Candidate sign and station kanji** (from the guides above, to confirm): 駅 (station), 入口 (entrance), 出口 (exit), 北口 / 南口 / 東口 / 西口 (north, south, east and west exits), 改札口 (ticket gate), 非常口 (emergency exit), 男 / 女 (restroom signs), 危険 (danger), 禁煙 (no smoking), 準備中 (shop not open yet), 円 (yen).
