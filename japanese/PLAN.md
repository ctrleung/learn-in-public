# Plan: survival Japanese for the trip (draft)

Draft, to adjust after the first diagnostic quiz and once the trip date is known. Short sessions, about 10 minutes or less, alongside the web plan. The schedule flexes with `gaps.md`; changes go in "Schedule changes" at the bottom.

## Main course

Genki (textbook and workbook) leads, with the Genki app for audio. Phrases, numbers and grammar follow Genki's order, and Renshuu and Anki are the daily practice. Numbers come early, since prices and times are among the most useful things on a trip.

## Phases

| Dates | Phase | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Now to 13 Dec | Recover hiragana | Diagnostic: what I still remember. Rebuild reading of hiragana from memory, with Renshuu doing the practice and this workspace testing it |
| 14 Dec to 17 Jan | Katakana, numbers, time, prices | Katakana for signs and menus. Numbers, times and prices for tickets and shopping. Start Genki (textbook, workbook, app audio) once hiragana reading is back. Lighter over the holidays |
| 18 Jan to 28 Feb | Survival phrases and questions | Greetings, polite requests, ordering, asking where and how much, following Genki |
| Final phase, until the trip | Signs, listening, practice | About a dozen sign and station kanji to recognize, listening practice with the Genki app, role-play of trip situations in chat |

## How each session works

1. Warm-up: 2 or 3 typed questions from due re-tests and open items in `gaps.md`.
2. Quiz first on the new item, then explain. A miss goes in `gaps.md`.
3. Explain it back in my own words, then a learning record with a Re-test date.

## Mastery gates

The dates are a guide; mastery sets the pace. Don't add a new row or group of characters until the previous one has passed its first re-test and stays correct at the next. Renshuu and Anki do the daily practice, and sessions here test it from memory, with items mixed across earlier rows. A phase is done when its key items are at the 7-day stage or higher and `gaps.md` has no open items for it; otherwise the phase extends and the change goes under "Schedule changes". Reviews always come before new material.

A phase is done when its review quiz scores about 85 percent (the usual range is 80 to 90) on top of the gate above, and it ends with a transfer check: use the items in a new combination, such as reading a word or phrase I haven't seen. With a fixed target date, a phase that needs longer means later phases shrink, and the change goes under "Schedule changes".
