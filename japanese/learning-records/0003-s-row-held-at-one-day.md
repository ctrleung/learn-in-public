# The s-row held at one day; the row pattern still lacks its exception

At the 1-day re-test the learner read all seven characters correctly in a scrambled check mixed with か and け (ka, su, se, shi, ke, so, sa), and explained how they tell す and し apart (the more complicated one comes first, so it's su) with すし as the anchor. Asked for the pattern behind the row, they stated that each sound starts with the consonant and that the vowels go a, i, u, e, o, noticing that this differs from English's a, e, i, o, u. They did not name the exception, し ("shi", not "si"). See [gaps.md](../gaps.md) and [reviews.md](../reviews.md).

## Evidence
- Scrambled read of seven characters, 7 of 7, from memory the day after teaching.
- す vs し answered correctly with the shape trick and the すし anchor.
- Row pattern stated in the learner's own words, but without the exception.

## Implications
- The s-row and す vs し move to a 3-day re-test; they only become fixed after a second correct re-test at a longer interval.
- Re-test the row pattern tomorrow, asking specifically which sound breaks it.
- Next new material is the t-row: た is already known, and the learner has the sounds for ち and つ but attached them to the wrong characters earlier. Keep mixing earlier rows into every test so the rows stay distinct.
- The learner draws useful comparisons with English (vowel order); use that when linking new ideas to what they already know.

Re-test: on or after 2026-11-22 for the row pattern; on or after 2026-11-24 for the s-row and す vs し.
