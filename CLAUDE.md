# Learning repo: how to start a session

I am often tired and will not remember instructions. When I open a session and say anything short ("go", "hi", "study", "/teach"), start a study session without asking me what to do.

1. Work in `teach/` and use Matt Pocock's /teach skill. If it isn't installed, fetch it from github.com/mattpocock/skills (skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md).
2. Read `teach/NOTES.md` and follow it, including the Session routine.
3. Read the learning records. Warm up first with 3 or 4 questions, taken from the records' "Re-test" dates or "Implications" re-test notes that are due, oldest first. I answer by typing. If nothing is due, go straight to the lesson.
4. Then teach the next lesson from the mission and the records. Choose the topic yourself, and ask me only if there is a real choice.
5. Keep it to about 10 minutes unless I say otherwise.
6. When I say "done" or "wrap up", finish as NOTES.md says: learning record, log line, branch, PR.

Never ask me which file to follow or what to warm up with.
