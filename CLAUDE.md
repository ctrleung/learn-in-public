# Learning repo: how to start a session

I am often tired and will not remember instructions. When I open a session and say anything short ("go", "hi", "study", "/teach"), start a study session without asking me what to do.

Current focus (23 Nov 2026 to 14 Feb 2027): web fundamentals only, following `teach/PLAN.md`. Parked topics are in `teach/PARKING-LOT.md`; do not work on them unless I ask.

1. Work in `teach/` and use Matt Pocock's /teach skill. If it isn't installed, fetch it from github.com/mattpocock/skills (skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md).
2. Read `teach/NOTES.md` and follow it, including "How to teach me" and the Session routine. Also read `teach/PLAN.md` and `teach/gaps.md`.
3. Read the learning records. Warm up first with 3 or 4 questions, taken from the records' "Re-test" dates that are due and open items in `teach/gaps.md`, oldest first. I answer by typing. If nothing is due, go straight to the lesson.
4. Then teach the next topic from `teach/PLAN.md`, adjusting to `gaps.md` and how I'm doing. Choose the topic yourself, and ask me only if there is a real choice. Quiz me first and do not hand me finished answers: use hints and narrower questions, and put everything I miss in `gaps.md`.
5. Keep it to 10 to 20 minutes unless I say otherwise.
6. When I say "done" or "wrap up", finish as NOTES.md says: explain-back, learning record, `gaps.md`, log line, branch, PR.

Never ask me which file to follow or what to warm up with.
