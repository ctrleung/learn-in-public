# Learning repo: how to start a session

I am often tired and will not remember instructions. When I open a session and say anything short ("go", "hi", "study", "/teach"), start a study session without asking me what to do.

This repo has one /teach workspace per subject:

- `web/`: active. Web fundamentals, 23 Nov 2026 to 14 Feb 2027, following `web/PLAN.md`. Sessions of 10 to 20 minutes.
- `japanese/`: active. Short sessions (about 10 minutes or less) that reinforce what I learn outside the repo (Anki for vocabulary, Renshuu for hiragana).
- `systems/`: dormant. Parked topics. Do not work on them unless I ask.

## Pick the workspace

1. If I name a workspace or a topic ("web", "japanese", "css", "aws"), use that one.
2. Otherwise, pick the workspace with the most overdue re-tests or open items in its `gaps.md`, and do not ask me. If nothing is due anywhere, use `web/`.
3. Work in that workspace's folder with Matt Pocock's /teach skill. If it isn't installed, fetch it from github.com/mattpocock/skills (skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md).

## Run the session

1. Read that workspace's `NOTES.md` and follow it, including "How to teach me" and the Session routine. In `web/`, also read `PLAN.md`.
2. Read its `gaps.md` and learning records. Warm up first with 3 or 4 questions (2 or 3 in `japanese/`), taken from the records' "Re-test" dates that are due and open items in `gaps.md`, oldest first. I answer by typing. If nothing is due, go straight to the lesson.
3. Then teach the next topic: from `PLAN.md` in `web/`, adjusting to `gaps.md` and how I'm doing. Choose the topic yourself, and ask me only if there is a real choice. Quiz me first and do not hand me finished answers: use hints and narrower questions, and put everything I miss in `gaps.md`.
4. If `japanese/MISSION.md` doesn't exist yet, the first Japanese session is a short mission interview instead of a lesson.
5. Keep it to the session length above unless I say otherwise.
6. When I say "done" or "wrap up", finish as that workspace's NOTES.md says: explain-back, learning record, `gaps.md`, log line (tagged with the workspace), branch, PR.

Never ask me which file to follow or what to warm up with.
